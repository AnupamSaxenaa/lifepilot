LifePilot

An offline-first productivity and focus app for Android.

LifePilot keeps your tasks, alarms and focus sessions working whether or not you have a connection, then syncs when you do. It includes an AI planner, focus rooms you can share with friends, and Mindful Lock for blocking distracting apps.

  • Offline-first tasks, lists and alarms
  • AI planning and coaching, metered with daily free credits
  • Deep focus sessions with streaks and statistics
  • Mindful Lock app blocking
  • Shared focus rooms and an opt-in leaderboard
  • Available in 21 languages
