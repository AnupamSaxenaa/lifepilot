Delete your LifePilot account

App: LifePilot (com.anupam.lifepilot) · Last updated: 25 August 2026

You can delete your LifePilot account and its data yourself, from inside the app. If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, use the email request at the bottom of this page.

This cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate. There is no recovery window, and the same account cannot be restored afterwards.

Option 1 — delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open LifePilot.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, then confirm again.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. If something goes wrong, the app will tell you and nothing will have been removed.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my LifePilot account”.

saxenaanupam2004@gmail.com

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may ask you to confirm your username so we delete the right account.

What is kept, and why

KeptReason
Purchase records, with your account link removed Required for financial accounting. After deletion these records no longer identify you.
Content you shared into a shared workspace, unlinked from your account It belongs to that workspace's remaining members too. We cannot delete other people's copies of something you shared with them. Your name is removed from it.
Workspaces you created that still have other members Ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member so their work is not destroyed. You are removed from the workspace.

Data stored only on your phone

LifePilot keeps a local cache so it works offline. Deleting your account clears it. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
